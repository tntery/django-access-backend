*VERIGATE SMART ACCESS CONTROL*
*Quick guide for portal users*

1. *Sign in*
Open the portal link provided by Belgravia Sports Club. Sign in with the username and password supplied by your portal administrator. There is no self-registration. Log out when finished, especially on a shared device. Never share your password.

2. *Find a user*
On *User Connections*, open *Quick actions*. Search by name, accounting user ID, display name, or access ID. You can filter by *Connected / Not Connected* or *Staff / Member*. Tap *Filter* to apply or *Clear* to reset. Tap *View* beside a result for its details.

3. *Understand the details*
• *Connected* means an access ID is linked; it does not guarantee access is granted.
• *Grant / Reject* shows the recorded access decision.
• Device *Active / Inactive* is a configured status, not a live connectivity check.
• *Auto* applies club rules: Staff are granted access; Members are checked against those rules.
• A manual *Grant* or *Reject* overrides Auto for all linked access IDs.
• Inactive devices and disconnected IDs still reject access. Offline devices receive changes at their next successful sync, so a change may not be immediate.
• Access history shows events attributed to that user; some attempts may not appear if they could not be attributed.

4. *Roles*
*Viewer* can view records and history. *Staff* can also manage mappings, access IDs, and access decisions. *Admin* can additionally manage portal users, settings, and user synchronisation. Your role is shown beside your username. Only make changes authorised by club procedures.

5. *Need help?*
Contact your club/portal administrator or Sesdeq support:
*Phone / WhatsApp:* +263 787 470 156
*Website:* https://sesdeq.co.zw

When reporting an issue, provide the user's name or accounting user ID, approximate attempt time, location/device if known, and the displayed decision. Do not send passwords. Share credential IDs or personal details only through a private, approved channel.
